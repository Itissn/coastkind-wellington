How to use this dataset

Use these fictional records to practise filtering, trend analysis, duplicate handling, quality checks and reward rules. Categories, sentiments, assessments and account activity were scripted, not collected from people or produced by an AI model.

Simulated alerts follow prototype thresholds. They do not describe actual pollution at these places. This balanced demonstration sample cannot estimate real pollution prevalence, model accuracy, water safety or government cost savings. Guest uploads permanently waive points; the points shown have no monetary value.
