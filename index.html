    BEFORE YOU HEAD OUT

    Useful coastal links

    Check official information, plan your day, and know where to report a concern. Each link opens the organisation's website in a new tab.

    SWIMMING · LAWA

    Check swimming water quality ↗

    Choose Wellington and your swimming spot to see monitoring results, predicted water quality and any warnings before you get in.

    lawa.org.nz     WEATHER · METSERVICE

    Plan around marine conditions ↗

    Check Kāpiti and Wellington marine forecasts, warnings and tides before paddling, boating or heading out to fish.

    metservice.com     REPORTING · GREATER WELLINGTON

    Report an environmental incident ↗

    Find the council's reporting contact and what details to provide when you notice pollution. Posting here does not notify the council.

    gw.govt.nz     RECREATIONAL FISHING · FISHERIES NEW ZEALAND

    Check the rules for your fishing area ↗

    Choose your fishing area and check current size and catch limits, closed areas and gear restrictions before each trip.

    mpi.govt.nz     BOATING · MARITIME NEW ZEALAND

    Get ready for a safer trip ↗

    Review the Boating Safety Code, preparation advice and guidance on equipment, communications and skipper responsibilities.

    maritimenz.govt.nz

    Community photos and AI suggestions cannot establish that water is safe. Use official water-quality information and local advice. These independent resources are not WAINET partners.
